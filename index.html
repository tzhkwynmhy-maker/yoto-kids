Welcome! 🎵

A simple, kid-friendly way to browse and play your family's Yoto library on a tablet, phone or computer.

  • Big picture tiles, so little ones can pick for themselves
  • Your groups, search, and a "recently played" row
  • Remembers where you left off in each story

Tap the button and sign in with your usual Yoto account.

🔒 You sign in on Yoto's own website, so this page never sees your password. It can only view and play: it can't change, add or delete anything in your Yoto account. Your library stays on your own device.

Tip: on an iPad or phone, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", to get an app icon.
